# Productive estates

The lord's estate shares the native compound project engine with castles, abbeys
and colleges. Its owner is the actual house or Crown account, not another chest.
New structures require locally adopted knowledge, consenting native ground,
delivered timber/stone, and actual construction wages. Political lordship alone
does not let a project displace another household's tenant rights.

`tickEstateWork`, after the current actor roll, maintains completed components
and pays existing residents for actual productive work. `tickEstates`, after
meals, completed projects and abbey gifts, decides whether to commission another
component. The site's expected receipts must cover its real worker's alternative
income, feeding, forgone land rent and construction over the component's useful
life. There is no year-based retry throttle or automatic cash income.

- A winepress receives the customary tenth of the actual vintage, pays its
  keeper, and gives the remaining wine to the original grower. Total wine does
  not increase. Its component cannot charge without actual processing labour.
- Parks and warrens refer to actual titled native fields and their paid
  boundaries. Their field interiors retain their terrain and ecological area;
  only the keeper's physical building and approach become occupied ground.
  Existing public tracks retain the native fence offsets/access convention.
  Ordinary assart selection excludes the enclosed field.
- Live deer, rabbits and pigeons belong to dated habitat state on those fields.
  Opening densities are explicit calibration inputs, not goods on sale. Natural
  growth is bounded by actual habitat; clearing removes habitat and live stock.
  A paid hunt consumes real live animals before crediting the native meat ledger.
- A dovecote captures four existing wild pigeons with a paid named worker.
  Stored live birds grow only as their owner consumes real grain. Its breeding
  stock is retained and only actual surplus birds become meat.
- A fishpond uses native stream/dam/topography and complete flood/access checks.
  The owner consumes actual fish to stock it. Its dated live population needs a
  full growing year; a paid native fisher harvests only stock above its breeding
  reserve. Drought, snow and unfunded upkeep reduce output.

Estate labour is actual daily hire, with one canonical household receiving the
wage and still having its ordinary mouths. A paid keeper cannot also take that
day's building/craft labour. Inactive hire releases the person's prior trade.
Native confiscation/succession changes the compound's owner only when all its
actual component titles and ground follow the new holder; it creates no coin or
goods. Published records and the settlement card report delivered quantities,
actual wages, current works and recorded live stocks without advancing biology.

Rent grain now tries the actual owner's covered grange/granary before a nearer
public facility. Carriage plans reserve real capacity, pay each owner’s carrying
bill once, then move only the quantity backed by actual net wages. No crew or
receipt leaves the goods exposed. Handling at the same point still takes labour.
This deliberately changes old carriage history and its report prices; the exact
ledger/report reuse tests hold the current paid model against both reducers.

The focused native-account fixtures cover stock depletion, loss of habitat,
actual wages, lost crew/tenant permission, method/access gates, pond maturity,
grain feeding, vintage title conservation and barn/carriage priorities. Combined
native save/speed/worker, conservation and drawing gates remain required before
publication. The game currently groups all game meat under `meat`; ecological
species and harvesting provenance remain distinct live resources. Opening
densities, carcass quantities and pond yields require later historical/economic
calibration. The native one-lake-per-settlement restriction remains.
