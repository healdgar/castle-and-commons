# Paid fabric and road work

The daily growth and church passes now make funding decisions each day. Ordinary houses, church stages, additional storeys, better fabric, widening, refits, roof maintenance and rebuilding of surviving ruins use saved `type: 'fabric'` projects. The calendar does not grant a finished building. Generated opening fabric still belongs to direct-era initialization and is not reported as a funded historical transaction.

`constructionRecipe` fixes labour, working days, timber and stone at commission. Adopted wheelbarrow/crane recipes reduce that labour and duration once and add their actual timber once. Supplies come from the actual payer's stock or real sellers. Only the amount actually paid by native `buildWorks` advances work. No crew means no progress. Materials already installed and wages already paid remain sunk when authority is lost.

Existing-building plans preserve native `idx`, geometry, structural church state and owner account in their validation snapshot. A surviving native ruin remains a ruin until its paid reconstruction finishes. Refits then move their actual household into that existing native building. Additional storeys affect native housing through the same building's tier. Purpose metadata grants no separate capacity. Roof repairs provide 360 days of service; a daily funding decision does not require rebuilding a roof daily.

A new house surveys the existing native frontage builder, saves its exact dimensions and street candidate, and rechecks that retained plan. It receives its native building index, real owner's title, occupied ground and household only at completion. Widening and church extensions reject structures on their line rather than demolishing unfunded neighbours. Fixed ground permission and payments use `storageGroundClaim` / `storageGroundPay`; these are shared receipt mechanics, not a transfer of an entire agricultural parcel. Geometry and title are revalidated during work and again before completion.

Parish/castle/friary church benefactions now use real transfers from the giving household or lord to the actual church fabric fund. The former annual gift quantity is spread over daily transfers. Abbey buildings are excluded from this parish funding pass: their institutional adapter owns their obligations. Local knowledge and adoption gate masonry churches, aisles, transepts/crossing towers, Gothic work, stone spires and masonry houses.

Road and paving orders use saved material quantities and the same actual-paid work fraction. Paving purchases and consumes the offered stone at either actual endpoint; it cannot seize pooled stone. Paving and road progress wait on actual worker wages. A new road checks its retained line against current native structures and ground permissions before paying and before installation. Non-prepaid `layRoad` now commissions a project. Completed roads occupy their paid native line and retain the paid ground licence.

The explicit recipe quantities are model parameters, not claims of calibrated medieval bills: new houses use `0.07 × footprint area` timber, ordinary rebuilding `0.8 × area` labour, additional storeys `0.6 × area` labour, widening `0.8 × added area`, annual vacant-roof repair `0.03 × area` labour plus `0.01 × area` timber, refits `0.12 × area` labour plus `0.025 × area` timber. Road bridges require 24 timber and 20 stone each; ordinary road work adds `0.002 × length` timber for posts and small works. Paving requires `0.12 × length` stone. Church labour and stone use the existing stage recipe, with explicit paid timber for scaffolds/roof work. Local prices and adopted paid tool recipes modify actual outlay.

Projection exposes planned geometry and structural church changes. Actual payers, frontage candidates, ownership snapshots and receipt records remain worker-side. Rendering neither chooses sites nor pays or completes a project.

Eleven small controlled source fixtures check native material receipts, native wages and cash conservation, church stage completion, no-crew stalls, changed-owner abandonment, retained-site obstruction, resumed actual fractional work, paid tool timber and endpoint paving stone. They do not prove native generation, full checkpoint reconstruction, worker parity, historical calibration or century-scale performance; those are serialized release gates run by the root agent.

A fully paid ground licence records its exact full native mask cells only after all required named receipts complete. Lawful parcel succession retains that footprint permission without a second compensation payment; a moved footprint into different cells is rejected. Ordinary owner projects capture the actual canonical household account, so a change of its head alone does not discard the funded contract.

Remaining construction migration in this batch's successor: town-history public works, new public lanes/extent, private backyard infill and other legacy instant institutional clients. The unused legacy `addBuildingLive` implementation is also due to retire. Ordinary frontage houses currently use already surveyed native frontage; they do not manufacture a free new street when frontage is exhausted.

Public lanes, town curtains and brush works now use the paid retained channel described in [FUNDED-PUBLIC-WORKS.md](FUNDED-PUBLIC-WORKS.md). A completed roof repair also cancels any previously queued native fall for that empty house.

Private repletion, changes of use and settlers' roofs now enter the same paid channel. A bounded daily infill decision surveys a side/rear cottage or an extension entirely within the parent building's actual native lot. A cottage uses the existing native compound-site public-approach validation with `inst:false`; it becomes an ordinary canonical residential building, whose native capacity and rent systems determine its use. The parent title/fabric is retained and checked throughout construction. Adjacent ground is still subject to the actual ground-holder permissions and paid footprint licence.

A market-driven change of use records the actual owner's fixed paid refit; it retains the building's occupants and architecture until completion. Speculative houses on an existing paid planned street require a named landlord, a real recipe and enough expected native rent to justify it. Completion supplies a roof; native migration supplies the families. Neither operation fabricates a household or shop income.

Street-shortening decisions now evaluate a bounded sample of current native household journeys daily and commission a retained paid public plan. The operative traffic estimate is a rolling daily quantity; it is not a cached geometry result. The old instant demolition, annual decision gate and multi-year retry cooldown are removed.

The focused fabric file now has fifteen cases. Its extra cases check paid architecture timing/occupants and native polygon containment for a prospective private cottage, alongside controlled completion geometry. Full native fit, actor-use, presentation and save gates remain serialized at the root. Automatic parish/friary/quarry/wharf foundations and later citadel work remain separate legacy clients pending migration; this document does not call those completed.
