# The wider Church: actual holders, service and jurisdiction

The continuation above parishes and abbeys uses canonical `corpFound('church', ...)` holders in `W.churchBodies`, with stable `church:N` keys. Diocesan, cathedral chapter, order General Chapter and papal chests are distinct. A sound native cathedral defines a diocesan seat; settlements belong to the nearest actual seat. All new holders start with empty purses. Creating a record grants no cash, goods, people, land, service or learning.

Domestic chests appear once in `accountBalances` and the native soak money census. The foreign papacy and General Chapter remain actual accounts outside that realm total. A transfer to or from them records the corresponding border flow, while a transfer between two foreign accounts changes neither realm inflow nor outflow. Native annual cash statements record actual receipts and payments, including separate parish fabric funds. Native graph save/history serialization retains these holders and references.

## Named service and daily order

`churchWork`, within early `tickAbbeyWork`, resolves cathedral seats and pays existing native clergy once for today's service. A bishop or dean is an actual living household head recruited from available skilled people. Recruitment excludes assigned field workers, existing institutional jobs, unfree people, spouses and absent people. His family retains its actual pantry and mortality. The office grants no population or second food allowance. Unfunded or absent service releases the office; new late appointments begin receiving wages the following day.

`tickChurchBodies`, at the end of late native `tickChurches`, handles assessments, household remittances, appointments, records and church fabric from actual balances. It does not add a new top-level simulation phase. Native marriage authorization reads today's paid service; probate runs after the deceased household repays its creditors. Native crop tithe allocation reads today's paid actual abbey vicar. Building changes remain native paid projects and become effective through the existing projects and map synchronization phases.

## Real dated payments

Each parish's customary annual diocesan assessment uses its closed previous-year cash receipts. Capital gifts, profession gifts, corrodies, loan advances, refunds and inheritance are excluded. Unpaid assessment remains an actual arrear; payment uses only existing parish money above its small poor-food reserve. A real share transfers onward to the separate cathedral chapter. The chapter can fund actual cathedral fabric from its spare purse.

Peter's Pence is collected on the native August 1 date by an actual funded bishop. Only actual households with a year's bread remaining pay. A household without the penny creates neither debt nor money. The initial currency conversion is an explicit game calibration: one twelfth of a day's bread is the customary penny in the game's units, rather than a claim that gold units equal historical pennies.

A papal crusade levy requires an explicit dated `churchTenthCall` mandate and actual available bishop; it does not arise merely because the calendar reached 1274. Maximum supported historical rates are one fortieth from 1199, one twentieth from 1215, and one tenth from 1274. The journaled crown-consent command supplies a term of one to six years. Actual closed recurring receipts are assessed once per mandate year; payment consumes existing institutional silver above actual operating bread or clergy reserves. Arrears survive expiry without a further assessment. The destination is the actual separate papal chest abroad.

Cistercian self-worked tithe privilege is recorded against the actual papal holder. The annual funded General Chapter journey remits real board/contribution to the separate order chest abroad, or real board to an actual visited mother house. No guessed levy or automatic order income is added.

## Parish appropriation and native rights

Benedictine and Augustinian adapters can receive a real patron's existing parish building title with an actual funded bishop's assent. They must have annual food and the actual vicar household's wage available, and the expected great tithe must support that service. A paid native vicar retains the parish's portion; the abbey receives only the remaining actual new grain. The ordinary parish fund and service remain separate from the abbey church. Unpaid or dead vicar service suspends great-tithe allocation to the abbey. Cistercians cannot appropriate a parish through this adapter. The current adapter deliberately cannot invent a patron's advowson where no native title establishes it.

## Marriage, wills and inheritance

Canon marriage eligibility uses actual parent links, with a seven-generation search and the reduced prohibited degree after 1215. First-degree relations cannot obtain a dispensation. Other prohibited collateral matches require today's actual funded bishop and a real household payment to the papal account; ordinary marriages can remain valid when no bishop has been appointed. Funded ecclesiastical records use bounded rolls and actual fees.

An actual pious adult under the native thirds custom may register a will directing the customary soul portion to an existing abbey or parish. It cannot divert the widow's, children's or creditors' shares. Native estate settlement resolves the authored recipient to its canonical account. Probate service draws a real fee only after native creditor repayment; lack of clergy or money does not create a charge or prevent the native estate from settling. After religious profession, a movable legacy passes to the actual abbey account and native land heirs exclude a professed personal holder.
