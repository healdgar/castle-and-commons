# Funded foreign expeditions and military brethren (#47, #41)

Native implementation; the release coordinator owns the combined world, worker, save/replay and performance gate. This is a bounded model of foreign holdings and campaign theatres, not a complete simulation of every overseas kingdom. The named regions are fictional holdings. Opening resources and crop quantities need calibration; no throughput or historically calibrated revenue claim is made here.

## Finite foreign holdings

`initForeignWorld()` runs once after the realm's actual opening households and accounts exist. Three independent named holdings have a ruler, actual family actors, family-owned fields and seed, a merchant chest and physical stores, a separate shipmasters' chest with named sailors and finite titled hulls, and a military fort with actual defenders and finite provisions. They are opening world data, with their opening coin/people/goods quantities recorded. They are never generated by a campaign or from a far-port price table. Foreign opening coin is excluded from the domestic money census.

Foreign daily food purchases circulate existing money between those real accounts and draw real stocks. Mortality and births change actual bounded family actors. A crop is made only on the annual harvest date from a worked, tool-equipped field and its finite retained seed; no fixed cash income is paid to a foreign ruler. Actual harvest sales and customary harvest rents move existing chests. The twentieth-day garrison wage date pays actual families; unpaid peacetime soldiers go back to their own holdings. Recruits are existing adults, paid from the actual treasury, with real tools consumed. No replacement army is generated on invasion. Hull types are part of opening physical assets; the passing calendar does not silently rebuild them.

`foreignAccount(a)` accepts only an exact registered foreign Church body, foreign holding/child account, or active registered expedition purse that has physically crossed the boundary. A `{foreign:true}` object grants no exception. `transfer` debits and credits the same actual accounts, records one domestic→foreign outflow or foreign→domestic inflow, and records none for foreign→foreign circulation.

## Preparation, absence and return

An optional, journaled decree requires the shared `foreignExpedition` capability, an actual adult living lord, an existing standing host with native actors, an owned open sea port reached by a real road, actual port workers, and enough finite hull capacity. Named shipmasters of a different, neutral foreign holding provide real transport. When a host exceeds capacity, its exact native men and finite horses are partitioned into a detachment; the remainder stays in the original host. Installed equipment is transferred only through the technology lane's actual partition helper; an engine is never cloned.

The full quoted money moves from the real sponsor into an expedition escrow. A short sponsor may mortgage a real eligible freehold/demesne field to a named, funded existing lender through `creditMortgage`; there is no external filler lender or unsecured invented advance. The quote covers physical food, local hull maintenance, actual loading wages, named foreign crew/charter costs, the actual host's pay, and a thirty-day bread reserve for a real overseas restock. Launch creates no spoils or profit.

The same lord notable or notable/head alias travels to the port. Arrival sets authoritative `h.at`; roads and sea travel take actual simulation days. Local loading consumes purchased timber/tools and pays actual port workers. Grain is bought and dispatched into the native custody ledger, whose actual cargo quantity is authoritative. Native families receive actual advance wages; existing foreign sell-swords have named registered foreign purses. Crew wages and victuals draw the named shipmasters' finite chest/stores. The same reserved vessels physically approach, berth, load and depart through the existing ship projection; commercial ship import/customs code does not process them a second time.

The expedition purse stays domestic until the actual sea boundary, then its remaining coin is recorded once as carried out. Payments and spoils while abroad are foreign→foreign transfers. At the actual returning boundary, only its remaining purse is carried in. No reward occurs when recall is ordered. Abroad, the same host is absent from native map battles and automatic household-host replacement. Head death uses native succession; it does not move a replacement heir abroad.

A siege uses actual remaining attackers, defenders, fort integrity and provisions, existing skills and the institutions RNG. Only actual completed equipment affects it, with physical wear. Casualties kill the same native people once. A victory takes only the actual defender treasury and finite merchant stocks; custody receives only goods removed from that named source. A defeat or withdrawal leaves those gains uncreated. Food loss and foreign purchase failure carry genuine casualty risk.

The same surviving host, lord and reserved hulls physically return. Remaining custody goods and coin go to the actual sponsor, once. The lord follows a real road home; cancelling a lord already on the road follows the travelled portion back. Cancelled or returning charter hulls remain unavailable until their physical home date. Religious brethren return to their actual native homes/jobs rather than remaining in a second paid host. Completed campaign history is recorded in bounded rolls; at most eight fully retired campaign bodies are retained alongside actual unfinished journeys/ships.

## Military religious corporations

Hospital and Temple formations consume `hospitalOrder`/`templeOrder` from the canonical technology seam. A real Church corporation with `bodyKind:'militaryOrder'` needs an actual completed chapel, an existing qualified unmarried native priest, at least two existing qualified unmarried lay fighters, physical tools/cloth and an actual named patron's endowment. Formation creates no people, proficiency, salary or automatic estate income. The same people receive canonical religious/vow/job markers and funded duties; their household accounts continue to buy their real bread. Daily service is paid only from the actual corporate chest or a real patron grant.

A funded order can send the exact native brethren with a campaign only when its real chest covers their food/wages and the already reserved hulls have actual spare capacity. Church staffing does not appoint a second bishop/dean into a military order. The canonical Church lane owns celibacy/inheritance recognition and actual papal assessment; no blanket tenth exemption is granted.

Temple suppression after 1312 is an explicit state transfer. The actual surviving Hospital body, otherwise the actual patron/Crown, receives real money, stocks, titles, liabilities and creditor books. A gone body resolves to that named successor only after the handover. Active brethren abroad return first; members, vows and paid duties are then actually reassigned or ended. There is no silently substituted borrower, erased debt or copied endowment.

## Daily order and controls

Royal borrowing and forward commission are ordinary daily decisions using saved bounded lender candidates, current funds and closed crop/real flock evidence, with an active-contract guard. Royal/corporate debt payments retain the genuine monthly twentieth-day accounting date; forward maturity, assessment and recoinage retain their real contractual/tenure dates. The obsolete annual forward marketing gate was removed. Daily wool proposals traverse actual abbey corporations, not every town household owner.

The crusade tithe summons the existing actual assessment/consent machinery for an overseas purpose. Actual refusal prevents the grant. Collection still waits for real assessment dates and real collectors; unavailable or poor households create arrears, not new cash. Crown cash remains fungible, and the same funded expedition operation spends it. `c-expedition`, `c-expedition-recall`, `c-crusade-tithe` and `c-military-order` are validated journal commands. `expeditionState()` and Crown/house cards are worker-built readonly views.

## Validation and source-private records

The focused `tools/foreign-expeditions.test.mjs` uses handcrafted small native/foreign records, never whole-world generation, Chrome or soaks. It covers exact foreign transfers and census boundaries, funded neutral hulls, native custody, real preparation wages/materials, finite captures, same-person casualties/return, road/ship recalls, finite detachment horses, actual order-member service, daily borrowing/forward eligibility, readonly reports and exact history under different daily batching. Actual Church foundation/suppression is additionally enabled when the coordinator merges the canonical Church registry; it remains a declared integration dependency here.

Source-private worker records needing explicit projection drift reasons:

- `W.foreignPolities`: canonical account, family/member, titled field/seed, merchant stock, fort/garrison, fleet/hull/master/crew records and bounded local rolls. Foreign actors have distinct `foreign:<holding>:<person>` IDs, not invented native PID/NID copies.
- `W.expeditions`: actual active escrow, sponsor, native army/head/body references, carrier/target, custody cargo (existing native accessor family), reserved vessel/crew refs, physical schedules, preparation/due/result records and bounded rolls. `W.expeditionN` is only the monotonic entity ID; finished records retire as above.
- `W.militaryOrders` points to the same canonical `W.churchBodies` corporations; it is an operation list, not another cash or population census. Members are the same native actor references and use `p.militaryOrder`, `p.religious`, `p.orderAppointed`, `p.orderPaid` and actual office/job markers.
- House/army journeys reference their actual expedition only while active. Native projected ships/travellers retain their ordinary physical records plus the source-only owner/contract references.
- Credit continuation: `s.creditList` contains actual bounded lender/charity pointers; `s.forwards`, `f.charges`, `W.credit.active/institutions` and linked `_owe` claim pointers are authoritative financial books. Readonly reports export balances/names/dates, not these private account identities.
