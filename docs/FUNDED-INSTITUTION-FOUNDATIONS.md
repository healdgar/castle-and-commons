# Paid institutional foundations

`tickInstitutionFoundations` makes bounded daily decisions immediately before the existing church pass. It checks current demand, authority, local capability, existing buildings, pending contracts and actual funds before surveying geometry. New parish churches, friary churches, quarries and port wharves commission saved `type: 'fabric', work: 'foundation'` contracts. Their old annual instant-completion branches in `tickHistory` are retired. Opening-era generation remains initialization and does not pretend to have funded construction history.

The contract retains its actual payer, beneficiary, exact footprint and approach, method, material quantities, fixed work quantity and native ground permission. Shared ground receipt, material installation and paid crew helpers run before native completion. Daily validation rechecks the retained geometry; it cannot choose a new free site when the old one becomes blocked. A changed settlement ruler abandons its ruler-authorized contract. A lawful succession to a paid ground parcel retains the exact licensed footprint through the shared receipt mechanism.

Parish foundations require both a nave and an adjacent irregular churchyard clipped to the actual native furlong. Worked land, existing lots, structures and routes remain protected. A fixed paid churchyard is supplied to the native church builder, which may retain that exact plot but may not replace it with an unpaid survey elsewhere. The approach must join a reachable native public street or an actual built highway. The church's structural state starts with the quoted nave and tower; Gothic work, aisles, transepts and spires remain later paid stages rather than calendar gifts. The native parish fund begins empty. Parish benefactions, worship and burial consume this same canonical church and churchyard.

Friary church eligibility is daily, with real local Franciscan (1210) or Dominican (1216) capability. Its order is saved on the actual church. This client supplies a worship building, not invented friars, endowment, a dormitory or productive estates. Those would require actual institutional membership and paid native components. Abbey foundations use their separate endowed shared-compound adapter and are not diverted into parish accounting.

A quarry requires actual rock or steep hillside, a dry fixed footprint and a passable connection to the town. Special quarry checks allow the geological slope that an ordinary house fitter rejects while protecting standing native buildings, churchyards, public ways, defensive circuits, historic ground and pending native sites. Opening or completed pits do not manufacture stone from a population coefficient. Up to three actual adult resident quarrymen work once per working day, observing the same Sunday rest as the native routines; the owner must pay their actual accounts before their present working strength produces stone for that owner. Owner self-work counts actual hands without a fictitious self-payment. An absent, besieged, unfunded or unstaffed pit produces no stone. Native institutional employment excludes abbey/estate jobs, school/tutor work, open study/apprenticeship and another craft employer; native `workers_` also excludes hosts and outlaw roles. A person assigned to those jobs cannot simultaneously supply quarry hands. Productive quantities remain an explicit model coefficient (`resource × 2.2 / 30 × working hands / 3`), not a historically calibrated output claim.

A wharf retains an actual linked public quay and its shoreline point, with a pier head in native deep sea water. Current quay geometry, public connection, water depth, structures, ground rights and the whole bounded pier footprint are checked during work. Completion installs an actual native wharf with the town's title; existing berth and shipping systems consume that building. Stone wharves require adopted local capability, actual stone and the paid recipe. The renderer only displays the completed native building or the existing paid project projection.

Recipe quantities are model parameters: church labour is `0.8 × area`, timber `0.1 × area` for timber churches or `0.035 × area` for masonry, with masonry stone `0.25 × area`; quarry construction is four annual food-price units of labour plus eight timber; wharf labour is `3 × length`, timber `0.8 × length` for timber or `0.4 × length` for masonry, with stone `1.5 × length`. Shared adopted machinery recipes add their real paid timber and fix labour/duration once at commission. Fixed construction work quantities are separate from daily funding decisions.

Seven light source fixtures exercise actual account transfers, materials and native crew payment with controlled geometry boundaries: no early map installation, parish yard/access rejection, later obstruction, actual quarry attachment, changed quay/depth rejection, no-worker stalls and quarry output requiring named paid workers. They do not certify native world generation, actor schedules, full save reconstruction or century-scale performance. Those remain the root agent's serialized release gates. The retained special footprint probes include both edges and corners at at most two-metre spacing; their semantics follow native terrain/route checks, not a second physics system.

Across-river New Town decisions now run daily inside growth. They require current demand for at least eight homes, insufficient existing frontage, an actual funded lord and a reachable existing native highway street crossing the river. A bounded straight branch on the far bank is surveyed without demolition and commissioned through the same retained paid public-street channel. It does not create an unpaid crossing, highway, household or roof. The `neu` frontage identity appears only on the actual completed street. The old annual instant New Town branch is retired. Citadels now also commission through a daily retained paid public plan; the old annual instant citadel branch is retired. Current local star-citadel capability, bastioned town walls, real strategic value and actual means are cheap guards before the bounded survey. The exact native enclosure pays for its parade ground, four nine-metre ramparts, four corner bastions, three separate longhouse barracks and approach. Barracks are native residential buildings consumed by the existing capacity and rehousing passes; no invented garrison or keep seat is added. Fixed owner and tenant compensation receipts must be paid before any priced native property is demolished. A new unpriced tenant, structure, pending project, protected churchyard, highway or changed street stops work. Existing local lanes crossing the enclosure retain their exact outside portions after the paid work, rather than vanishing wholesale. All quoted materials and actual native construction workers are paid through the shared helpers. The three barracks are aligned along the fort's depth so their long dimensions do not overlap at their eighteen-metre spacing. Six tiny fixtures exercise retained native client geometry, source completion, named receipts/materials/wages, no-worker refusal, new tenant/project refusal, exact outside-lane preservation and a crossing highway with endpoints outside. The native citadel now joins `fortCircuits`: its siege sectors derive from the same square, rampart and bastion geometry. Movement blocks the actual nine-metre ramparts and solid corner works, passes the six-metre gate for friendly actors and roads, and obeys the existing native breach thresholds. Drawing reads that same geometry and opening. The replaced town curtain is removed from its canonical `wallKind` sectors at structural completion; it does not reappear for free when the citadel is later razed. No salvage cash or stock is invented. Existing saved/opening citadels acquire the same native circuit at structural preparation. The direct opening initializer also uses the corrected separate barracks orientation. This is an intentional model repair, not an exact optimization of the old invisible-wall behavior. Five light source fixtures check native circuit installation, permanent original-curtain removal, friendly/hostile gate passage, rampart/bastion barriers, actual damage breaches, drawing's identical opening and route certificate invalidation on equal-count pose/size/razing edits. Full generated-world save/speed/render checks remain root's serialized gates.


Courtyard-house consolidation now makes daily private investment decisions after current adopted masonry, town size, actual wealth and pending work guards. The richest prospective owners in the town's dearest fifth may acquire two to four adjacent native residential plots. A retained recipe fixes labour (`0.8 × new roof area`), timber (`0.04 × area`), stone (`0.25 × area`) and at least sixty paid days. The candidate rectangle must fit the exact union of those native convex home plots; polygon subtraction rejects narrow unpaid gaps and interior holes, rather than sampling them away. Construction never marks standing homes removed during survey. All acquired buildings reserve their pending demand once.

Saved named owner and tenant receipts precede private title transfer. Real owner purchase receipts are recorded as land transactions, tenant payments as displacement compensation, and construction wages remain separate. Acquired homes remain standing and habitable if a later material, crew, tenant or site problem stops the rebuild. Their purchased canonical titles stay with the actual buyer; they are not left with the compensated former owner during inheritance. Current purchased geometry and new unpriced tenants are checked each day. Native demolition and the new courtyard roof happen only after the full fixed work and materials are paid. The real payer household's current living head receives its new native home, while displaced households use the existing rehousing model and the ordinary native capacity function. No extra rooms, family or people ledger is invented. Opening generation is unchanged. Five focused source fixtures cover no early cash/title/map changes, actual purchase receipts and title transfer, a narrow unpaid plot gap, absent workers and changed paid plots/new tenants. Root owns generated-world/save/speed integration testing.

Courtyard consolidation also retains the actual reachable public street and a fixed private door passage at commission. Each daily check verifies its endpoint remains on that live public street and connected to the native network, and that native water, fort, slope and standing footprints leave the passage clear. Only the priced acquired homes are ignored as eventual removals. Exact polygon subtraction confines its 1.5 m corridor to those acquired native plots and the public street's existing half-width plus the native parcel solver's 0.8 m reserved frontage margin; an empty unrelated toft cannot grant passage. The retained path joins the shared exact ground certificate and pending reservation, and the completed court refers to the same native street. This intentionally prevents inaccessible rebuilds; it adds no free public lane. Focused source fixtures cover a stopped/disconnected/moved/private endpoint, new standing obstruction, unrelated unbuilt toft, and current water/fort/slope constraints.
