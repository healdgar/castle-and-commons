# Knightly service and institutions (#41)

Native implementation, awaiting the coordinator's integrated gate. This intentionally changes medieval history; one-day, worker, fast-forward, save and replay must still execute the same operations in the same order.

A great house is the same account through succession. The existing first living heir now inherits even when a minor. A dated relief is assessed from the house's observed annual receipts at the customary one-twelfth rate; unresolved earlier relief remains due on the same house. The realm's existing legal majority remains sixteen. Wardship takes only realised positive estate surplus after actual monthly military expenses, preserving the same operating chest as ordinary household spending. Death, majority, expiry and extinct ward buyers end or revert the actual right. A named buyer bids only when its actual steward advantage, less maintenance, exceeds the Crown's expected remaining income, and pays from its actual chest.

A royal war saves each annual call. A house sends its existing host for forty days or pays actual scutage from annual receipts. The cost comparison uses the host's actual rate and local food price. No house can do both for the same call. Once parliament exists, the existing real attendance, wages, travel and voting mechanism decides commutation consent; refusal prevents collection and does not generate a movable-wealth grant. Active service explicitly makes that house a royal ally in native military hostility; expiry ends that alliance and recalls the actual host. Private feuds remain separate.

A tournament requires at least two actual living, skilled notable riders with actual titled horses. Real local carpenters build the lists through paid daily work and purchased, consumed timber/tools. The prize is transferred into an actual escrow chest before proclamation. The riders travel as the same native notable or notable/head alias, with actual arrival, board and return. Bouts use riding/arms and the institutions RNG stream. The loser pays an actual horse ransom or conveys one actual horse title. The escrow pays the winner, or refunds the real host on cancellation/no attendance. Unperformed work and unavailable prizes create no receipts, horses, or actor copies. The former 700-coin petition stand-in commissions this same operation.

Paid men may remain together after a war. A company retains the exact discharged actors and finite horses, with a named native captain and initially empty company purse. Actual members can contribute bread money beyond their families' subsistence. Existing foreign sell-swords who stay become the same resident actors with actual household accounts; no second population is generated. Hiring needs a named employer at the company's actual town, a funded customary month's wage, and local food costs. Native houses/Crown skip ordinary host wages for companies, whose exact contract wages flow employer → company → soldiers' actual households. Unemployed hungry men join the native outlaw system as the same people; dissolution returns actual people, finite horses and remaining money once.

A chivalric order after 1348 requires a real chapel, actual lettered/scriptural celibate head and actual founder funds. Paid chapel services appoint the real chaplain; an empty title creates neither priest nor income. Membership is recorded only for actual adult riders at the chapel whose houses pay the dues. Annual accounts pay actual services and record actual members' military service, with a surviving order chest included in cash reports.

The shared technology seam owns knightService, scutage, tournament, licensedTourney, mercenaryCompany and chivalricOrder. A calendar fallback exists only before technology world initialization in isolated fixtures/legacy adapters. Capabilities authorize consideration, never supply money, people, skills, horses or materials.

knightState() is a worker-built readonly report. Crown/house controls for service choice, scutage custom, ward offers, lists, company hire and orders are validated and journaled. accountBalances() includes actual company/order chests and tournament escrow. The roll is bounded; active records contain real contracts and actor/account references.
